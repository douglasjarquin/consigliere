Agent lifecycle & telemetry

How a soldier is born, checked on, and retired, and the optional per-turn record that lets you replay a long session.

Lifecycle

spawnWorkspace created by herdr; soldier launched with the root session’s harness.
workThe soldier commits inside its worktree. Status is read from herdr, never from the pane.
checkpointBounded, foreground. The consigliere reviews and decides: continue, redirect, or land.
stopNormal completion, or the Stop-hook backstop when a soldier exits outside the protocol.
retireOnly after work has landed. Unlanded work is never torn down.

Turn telemetry

Optional. Records per-turn timing and outcomes for a session so supervision can be inspected after the fact. Written under data/, disposable.
