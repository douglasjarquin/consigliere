Design system

Noir editorial. A serif for what the boss reads, a monospace for what the machine reads, one taupe accent, and hairlines instead of boxes.

Principles

01

Two voices, two faces

Prose is serif. Anything a terminal would print, or a label a machine would read, is mono.

02

Hairlines, not cards

Structure comes from 1px rules and grid gutters. Filled surfaces are reserved for code and one band per page.

03

One accent, sparingly

Taupe marks eyebrows, keys, active states and the emphasised word in a headline. Never fills.

04

Dark is home; light is a switch

Every color is a token on body. The toggle flips one attribute; nothing else changes.

Color

Seven tokens per mode. Whites and blacks are warm with chroma under 0.02; the accent is the only chromatic value.

Forms

Inputs are underlines, not boxes: transparent background, 1px bottom rule, accent on focus. Labels are mono caps above. Errors are a muted sentence beneath, never red. Checkboxes are 14px squares; a filled square means checked.

Voice

Do

Use the family's nouns as terms of art: boss, consigliere, capo, soldier, land, the books. Keep them lowercase in prose and mono when they are paths. State what the system refuses to do as flatly as what it does.

Don't

No movie quotes, no accents, no violence. The metaphor is an org chart, not a threat. No exclamation marks; no emoji; no gradients.
