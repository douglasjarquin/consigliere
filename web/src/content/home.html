Consiglieren. — the boss's counsel

You describe the work. You give the word. Nothing else is yours to carry.

Launch codex or claude in this repo and it becomes the consigliere: the boss's single point of contact for all software work, delegating everything to autonomous soldiers it spawns, supervises, and lands work from.

It is a from-scratch personal rewrite of Firstmate, built for two harnesses and one terminal runtime, leaning on their native features instead of generic multi-backend shims. Soldiers inherit the root session's harness, so one consigliere works wherever you work: codex at home, claude at work.

It never merges without you. Landing is always your call.

Every soldier gets its own herdr worktree. Every session opens with one ordered digest of the whole operating picture. Every decision that lands code passes through you; yolo lets it answer routine review decisions alone, but nothing lands without the boss.

Roughly nine thousand lines of bash. One harness layer, one supervision protocol, one Stop-hook backstop.
